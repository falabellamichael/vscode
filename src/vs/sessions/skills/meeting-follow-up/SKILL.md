# Meeting Follow-Up

Start with the notes, transcript, or conversation the user supplied. If there is no meeting material, ask for the notes or the specific meeting to use.

Lead with what the group decided. Keep proposals and unresolved disagreements under open questions. Include the reason for a decision when the record gives one.

For each agreed action, capture the deliverable and any stated owner or deadline. Someone suggesting an idea has not necessarily volunteered to do it. If ownership or timing matters and is unclear, flag the missing detail. Preserve relative deadlines when the meeting date is unknown.

Match the requested audience and format. For an email or message, draft the follow-up in chat. Include only sections that have content.
